How It Works

Up and running in 60 seconds.

Simple setup designed to get you organized and focused immediately.

01

Create Your Account

Sign up with your name, email, and password. Your personal account keeps your data safe and private.

02

Confirm Your Email

Open the confirmation link sent to your registered email to activate your account and verify your identity.

03

Personalize Your Workspace

Choose your primary goals, select the modules that fit your life, and set up your habit routines.

04

Plan & Track Daily

Start each day with clear tasks, track your habit streaks, log focus sessions, and record notes.

05

Review & Improve

Gain insights from your personal analytics and weekly summaries to make steady, compounding progress.

Security & Trust

Your data stays yours.

Built with privacy, security, and performance at its foundation.

🔐

Secure Authentication

Enterprise-grade authentication with secure email confirmation, encrypted credentials, and protected session tokens.

✉️

Email Verification

Every account is verified through a direct email confirmation link to prevent unauthorized registrations.

🔒

Isolated User Data

Each user's data is stored in a private, isolated container. No user can ever access another user's personal information.

🚦

Rate Limiting & Protection

Built-in brute-force protection and rate-limited authentication endpoints safeguard your account.

⚡

Fast & Offline Ready

Designed for fast load times and local responsiveness, so you can track habits and tasks anywhere.

🛡️

XSS & Input Sanitization

Comprehensive sanitization protects all notes, tasks, reflections, and inputs throughout the entire workspace.
